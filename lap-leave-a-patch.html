The science

Doing less is
doing a lot.

Pollinators are running out of flowers to eat. A permanent clover patch is more useful than one skipped mow — it feeds bees for five months straight, and feeds the soil while it's at it.

100+
bee species forage on white-clover lawns — honeybees, bumblebees, mason & sweat bees.
Top 3
White clover ranks among the top nectar sources for honeybees in North America.
May–Sep
Five months of continuous bloom — forage right through the season, not one weekend.
0 lbs
Clover fixes its own nitrogen (80–150 lbs/acre), so a patch needs no fertilizer.

Sources: Univ. of Minnesota Extension (bee lawns); Lerman et al. — reduced mowing raises bee abundance; USDA/OSU Extension on clover as pollinator forage. Note: leaving a lasting patch is better supported than a one-month "no-mow," whose original 2020 study was later retracted.

How it works · three steps

Touch grass.
Then let it be.

01

Pick a patch

A corner, a strip, a whole square meter (about 11 sq ft). Draw the line and stop mowing inside it. That's the hard part done.

02

Let it bloom

White clover is the MVP — drought-proof, bee-approved, and it feeds the soil. Overseed a handful if your patch is bare.

03

Tag it

Post your patch with #LeaveAPatch. Screenshots of you doing nothing, for a cause. Peak efficiency.
